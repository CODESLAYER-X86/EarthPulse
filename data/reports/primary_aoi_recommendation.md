# Primary AOI Recommendation: Potengi Estuary (25 km²)

## 1. Recommendation Summary
We recommend the **25 km² window** (500 x 500 pixels at 10m resolution) centered on the Potengi Estuary, Natal, Brazil. 

### Selected Coordinates (EPSG:32725)
- **Row Slice:** `1480:1980`
- **Column Slice:** `13611:14111`
- **Easting Range:** `252,755.00` to `257,755.00`
- **Northing Range:** `9,360,355.00` to `9,365,355.00`

## 2. Justification
1. **Ecological Representation:** Fully contains the documented mangrove stands and the primary estuarine channel of the Potengi River.
2. **Data Integrity:** Exhibits high three-date common-valid coverage and robust HH/HV finite-positive backscatter.
3. **NASA Space Apps Suitability:** Provides a manageable dataset size for rapid processing, visualization, and validation against Global Mangrove Watch and Sentinel-2 data.
