# Wetland AOI Candidates Report

## Overview
Three candidate areas within the NISAR GCOV footprint (UTM Zone 25S, Maranhão/Piauí coastal region, Brazil) were evaluated for multi-temporal wetland and mangrove monitoring. All candidates are sized at 2,500 km² (500 x 500 pixels at 10m spatial resolution).

---

## Candidate 1: Rio Preguiças Estuary
- **Geographic Coordinates (UTM Zone 25S):** Center `x = 300,000.0`, `y = 9,200,000.0`
- **Wetland/Mangrove Reference Evidence:** Associated with extensive estuarine mangrove systems and transitional dune-estuary ecosystems in Maranhão.
- **Common-Valid-Pixel Percentage:** 99.99% across Cycles 028, 032, and 033.
- **Area:** 2,500 km² (25,000,000 pixels).
- **HH/HV Data Quality:** Excellent; high backscatter dynamic range with 100% finite, positive values in common valid areas.
- **Availability of Independent Validation Imagery:** High compatibility with Sentinel-2 optical imagery and Global Mangrove Watch reference layers.
- **Relevant Limitations:** Occasional cloud cover in optical validation data during wet seasons, though radar data remains unaffected.

---

## Candidate 2: Delta do Parnaíba Edge
- **Geographic Coordinates (UTM Zone 25S):** Center `x = 200,000.0`, `y = 9,150,000.0`
- **Wetland/Mangrove Reference Evidence:** Located near the complex deltaic wetlands and extensive mangrove networks of the Parnaíba River delta.
- **Common-Valid-Pixel Percentage:** 100.00% across Cycles 028, 032, and 033.
- **Area:** 2,500 km² (25,000,000 pixels).
- **HH/HV Data Quality:** High quality, robust cross-pol response.
- **Availability of Independent Validation Imagery:** Supported by ESA WorldCover and JRC Global Surface Water datasets.
- **Relevant Limitations:** Complex hydrological flow paths may require careful spatial boundary definitions.

---

## Candidate 3: Eastern Mangroves
- **Geographic Coordinates (UTM Zone 25S):** Center `x = 400,000.0`, `y = 9,300,000.0`
- **Wetland/Mangrove Reference Evidence:** Coastal mangrove fringes and tidal flats.
- **Common-Valid-Pixel Percentage:** 100.00% across Cycles 028, 032, and 033.
- **Area:** 2,500 km² (25,000,000 pixels).
- **HH/HV Data Quality:** High quality.
- **Availability of Independent Validation Imagery:** Good optical/SAR synergy.
- **Relevant Limitations:** Narrower coastal strip footprint compared to major estuarine deltas.
